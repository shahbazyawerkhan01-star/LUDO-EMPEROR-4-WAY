============================================================
  LUDO EMPEROR 4 WAY  -  FINAL GAME
============================================================

Everything is in this folder. Nothing to install.


------------------------------------------------------------
  PLAY IT RIGHT NOW  (no setup, no internet, no Node.js)
------------------------------------------------------------

  On a PC / laptop      ->  double-click   PLAY-WEB.html
  On a phone / tablet   ->  double-click   PLAY-MOBILE.html

  Each file is the WHOLE game. Copy it to a phone, email it,
  put it on a USB stick - it works on its own.

  NOTE: leave index.web.html and index.mobile.html alone. Those
  are developer files; double-clicking them does nothing.


------------------------------------------------------------
  PLAY ON SEVERAL DEVICES AT ONCE  (optional)
------------------------------------------------------------

  Double-click   PLAY.cmd
  (needs Node.js from nodejs.org - only for this option)

  The black console window prints two addresses:

      this PC :  http://127.0.0.1:8080/
      phone   :  http://<your-ip>:8080/

  Keep that window open while playing; close it to stop.
  Bookmark the link and you can come back any time.

  Phone cannot connect?
      Double-click ALLOW-PHONE-ACCESS.cmd and accept the
      Administrator prompt. That opens the port for your own
      Wi-Fi only - nothing is exposed to the internet.


------------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT IS IN HERE
------------------------------------------------------------

  PLAY-WEB.html ......... the whole game, tuned for PC browsers
  PLAY-MOBILE.html ...... the whole game, tuned for phones
  PLAY.cmd .............. starts the shared server (web + phone)
  ALLOW-PHONE-ACCESS.cmd  one-time firewall helper for phones
  START-HERE.md ......... full guide: rules, landscape, fixes
  src/ .................. the source code
  dist/ ................. served build (used by PLAY.cmd)
  dist-web/ dist-mobile/  the two single-file builds
  Run with local server/  the server and its own launcher



------------------------------------------------------------
  QUICK TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------

  * Turn a phone sideways for the wide board layout.
  * Your turn is two taps: tap a NUMBER, then tap a glowing
    PIECE. A hand points down at what to tap next.
  * You must take EVERY capture your roll allows - with 5 + 5
    you can take two opponents in one turn.
  * Black screen? You opened a developer file. Use PLAY-WEB.html
    or PLAY-MOBILE.html.

  Full details, including the capture rule and troubleshooting,
  are in START-HERE.md
